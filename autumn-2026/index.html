🇸🇮 Soča — Valley of the Emerald River

670 km 7-8 hrs drive approx. 8,000 CZK

Route from Prague

  • Prague (Na Petynce) → Tábor → České Budějovice → Dolní Dvořiště
  • AUSTRIA: Linz → Salzburg (bypass) → Villach
  • SLOVENIA: Jesenice → Kranjska Gora → Vršič Pass → Trenta → Soča

Budget & Fees

  • Austria Vignette (10 days) 300 CZK (12 €)
  • Slovenia Vignette (1 week) 400 CZK (16 €)
  • Diesel (1450 km, 78 l) 3,800 CZK
  • Camping / Stellplatz 1,200 CZK
  • Food & Supplies 1,800 CZK
  • Parking 500 CZK
  • TOTAL approx. 8,000 CZK

Day-by-Day Program

Sep 17 (Day 1)
  • Drive to Slovenia, Kranjska Gora, iconic Vršič Pass, descent to Trenta, sleep in the car.
Sep 18 (Day 2)
  • Great Soča Gorge (Velika Korita), Small Soča Gorge (Mala Korita), hiking along the emerald river, overnight stay.
Sep 19 (Day 3)
  • Mangart Saddle or Planina Blato, views of the alpine ridges, start the return journey in the evening.
Sep 20 (Day 4)
  • Arrival home at night / early morning, unpacking, and resting.

Stellplätze & Car Sleeping

  • Camp Trenta Area: Quiet official parking by the river.
  • Kamp Klin (Lepena): Confluence of Soča and Lepenjice, tree shade.
  • Vršič Pass Parking: Option to sleep before the ascent on designated platforms.
  • PZZ Bovec: Caravan parking with water and electricity hookup.
  • Below Mangart Saddle: Wilder spots at mountain rest areas.

Wildlife in the Area

Deer 4/5
Chamois (jumpy mountain goat) 5/5
Wild Boars 2/5
Birds of Prey 4/5

Information for Dogs (Staffbull friendly)

Entry Requirements: Valid Pet Passport, microchip, rabies vaccination.
Movement in the Park: Dogs must be kept on a leash in Triglav National Park.
Muzzle: Have one ready in your backpack (narrow footbridges).
Recommendation: Water from the Soča River is drinkable but very cold. Keep a towel in the car!

🇸🇮 Logarska Dolina — Solčavsko & The Alps

580 km 6-7 hrs drive approx. 7,500 CZK

Route from Prague

  • Prague (Na Petynce) → Tábor → České Budějovice → Dolní Dvořiště
  • AUSTRIA: Linz → Steyr → Liezen → Leoben → Graz
  • SLOVENIA: Maribor → Celje → Solčava → Logarska Dolina

Budget & Fees

  • Austria Vignette 300 CZK
  • Slovenia Vignette 400 CZK
  • Diesel (1250 km, 68 l) 3,500 CZK
  • Camping / Stellplatz 1,200 CZK
  • Food & Supplies 1,800 CZK
  • Parking & Valley Entry 300 CZK
  • TOTAL approx. 7,500 CZK

Day-by-Day Program

Sep 17 (Day 1)
  • Drive to Slovenia, pass through Solčava, entry into Logarska Dolina, Rinka Waterfall, overnight stay.
Sep 18 (Day 2)
  • Hiking through the valley to Okrešelj, mountain meadows, observing deer rutting, overnight stay.
Sep 19 (Day 3)
  • Solčava Panoramic Road, views of the Kamnik-Savinja Alps, relaxing by the Savinja River, evening departure.
Sep 20 (Day 4)
  • Arrival home at night or early morning.

Stellplätze & Car Sleeping

  • PZZ Logarska Dolina: Designated parking at the beginning of the valley.
  • Eko Kmetija Povšnar: Quiet farmyard for a small fee.
  • Solčava Panoramic Road: Scenic rest areas.
  • Camper Stop Luče: Just below the valley by the Savinja River.
  • Kamp Šmica: Option to park near the forest.

Wildlife in the Area

Deer 5/5
Chamois (jumpy mountain goat) 4/5
Wild Boars 3/5
Birds of Prey 4/5

Information for Dogs (Staffbull friendly)

Entry Requirements: Pet Passport, microchip, valid rabies vaccination. No breed bans.
Movement in the Park: Keep dogs on a leash in the nature reserve due to the deer f**king season (they're angry and horny).
Recommendation: Wonderful gentle valley trails without sharp stones. Gentle on paws.

🇦🇹 Gesäuse — Enns River Gorge

420 km 5 hrs drive approx. 6,500 CZK

Route from Prague

  • Prague (Na Petynce) → Tábor → České Budějovice → Dolní Dvořiště
  • AUSTRIA: Linz → Steyr → Nationalpark Gesäuse

Budget & Fees

  • Austria Vignette 300 CZK
  • Diesel (950 km, 51 l) 2,700 CZK
  • Camping / Stellplatz 1,200 CZK
  • Food & Supplies 1,800 CZK
  • Parking 500 CZK
  • TOTAL approx. 6,500 CZK

Day-by-Day Program

Sep 17 (Day 1)
  • Arrival in the region, drive through the Enns Gorge, light evening hike around the river, overnight stay.
Sep 18 (Day 2)
  • Hiking in Gesäuse National Park, climbing through mountain forests, observing chamois (jumpy mountain goat), overnight stay.
Sep 19 (Day 3)
  • Johnsbach, mountain meadows, iconic views of the limestone walls of Hochtor, evening return.
Sep 20 (Day 4)
  • Relaxed drive back to the Czech Republic.

Stellplätze & Car Sleeping

  • Wohnmobilstellplatz Gstatterboden: Heart of the national park.
  • Parkplatz Weidendom: Visitor center, quiet overnight parking.
  • Johnsbach Ebnerkreuz: Parking below trailheads.
  • Camping Forstgarten: Nature campsite right by the Enns riverbed.
  • Admont Parkplatz: Alternative near the monastery to refill water.

Wildlife in the Area

Deer 3/5
Chamois (jumpy mountain goat) 5/5
Wild Boars 2/5
Birds of Prey (Eagles) 5/5

Information for Dogs (Staffbull friendly)

Entry Requirements: Austria has no nationwide restrictions for Staffbulls. Pet Passport required.
Movement in the Park: Strict ban on roaming off-leash. Leash is a must.
Recommendation: Beware of steep climbs in scree fields - protect paw pads.

🇦🇹 Hochschwab — Kingdom of the Chamois (jumpy mountain goat)

470 km 5-5.5 hrs drive approx. 6,800 CZK

Route from Prague

  • Prague (Na Petynce) → Tábor → České Budějovice → Dolní Dvořiště
  • AUSTRIA: Linz → Ybbs → Mariazell → Hochschwab

Budget & Fees

  • Austria Vignette 300 CZK
  • Diesel (1050 km, 57 l) 2,900 CZK
  • Camping / Stellplatz 1,300 CZK
  • Food & Supplies 1,800 CZK
  • Parking 500 CZK
  • TOTAL approx. 6,800 CZK

Day-by-Day Program

Sep 17 (Day 1)
  • Arrival in Seewiesen area, light hiking along the foothills of the limestone mountain range, overnight stay.
Sep 18 (Day 2)
  • Ridge mountain meadows, scenic routes, guaranteed observation of chamois (jumpy mountain goat) herds, overnight stay.
Sep 19 (Day 3)
  • Emerald mountain lakes, alpine pastures, autumn landscape photography, evening departure.
Sep 20 (Day 4)
  • Arrival home.

Stellplätze & Car Sleeping

  • Bodenbauer Parkplatz: Iconic starting point for ascents.
  • Seewiesen Seebergsattel: Mountain pass, flat parking spots.
  • Tragöß Grüner See Area: Close to the famous lake.
  • Wohnmobilstellplatz Aflenz: Quiet municipal parking.
  • Wildalpen Camping: On the northern side by the Salza River.

Wildlife in the Area

Deer 4/5
Chamois (jumpy mountain goat) 5/5
Wild Boars 2/5
Birds of Prey 4/5

Information for Dogs (Staffbull friendly)

Entry Requirements: Pet Passport, microchip, vaccination. Styria has no restrictions for SBT.
Movement on Pastures: Attention! Wild free grazing cows. Watch out for free-grazing cows.
Recommendation: Attention! No woa! Woa sources are missing on the ridge plateaus — bring plenty of woa.

🇭🇺 Őrség — Quiet Forests & Wetlands

470 km 5-5.5 hrs drive approx. 5,800 CZK

Route from Prague

  • Prague (Na Petynce) → Jihlava → Brno → Břeclav
  • AUSTRIA: Vienna (bypass)
  • HUNGARY: Szombathely → Őriszentpéter → Őrség

Budget & Fees

  • Hungary Vignette (10 days) 400 CZK (16 €)
  • Diesel (1000 km, 54 l) 2,800 CZK
  • Camping / Stellplatz 1,000 CZK
  • Food & Supplies 1,300 CZK
  • Parking 300 CZK
  • TOTAL approx. 5,800 CZK (Cheapest)

Day-by-Day Program

Sep 17 (Day 1)
  • Arrival in Őrség National Park, quiet forest trails, meadows, and wetlands, overnight stay.
Sep 18 (Day 2)
  • Cycle paths and hiking trails through dense pine forests, intense observation of deer rutting, overnight stay.
Sep 19 (Day 3)
  • Quietest remote parts of the park, autumn nature photography, late afternoon departure.
Sep 20 (Day 4)
  • Return home. Cuddle. Rest.

Stellplätze & Car Sleeping

  • Őriszentpéter Forest Spot: Quiet forest rest area.
  • Szalafő Pityerszer Parking: Near the folk architecture skansen.
  • Vadása-tó Camping: By the forest lake.
  • Velemér Forest Edge: Absolute peace at the forest edge.
  • Magyarszombatfa Spot: Near the border forests.

Wildlife in the Area

Deer (they wanna f**k) 5/5
Chamois (jumpy mountain goat) 1/5
Wild Boars (help, i'm afraid, pooping) 5/5
Birds of Prey 3/5

Information for Dogs (Staffbull friendly)

Entry Requirements: Pet Passport, microchip, valid vaccination. No specific breed restrictions.
Terrain: Flat terrain and pine needles — absolutely ideal for long, relaxing walks (good for ur pelvis).
Warning: September is the peak of the deer rut and wild boar activity — be careful babe (NO YOLO)!
